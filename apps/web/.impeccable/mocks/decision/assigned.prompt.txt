A high-fidelity desktop website homepage design (landing page UI, 1536x1024 landscape) for "PHOTNO", a private photo-delivery platform for professional photographers in Egypt. The page is designed like a white-cube photography exhibition room.

Layout, top to bottom:
1. Slim top navigation bar across the full width on a deep forest green (#344e41) wall: left the white wordmark "✱PHOTNO" (a star mark followed by bold serif-less capitals), center small uppercase museum-wayfinding links "How it works", "Pricing", "For clients", right a small outlined "Log in" and a solid pale sage (#c7dcc9) button "Request access".
2. Hero occupying most of the viewport: the whole background is a flat deep forest green gallery wall (#344e41) lit from above by a soft warm brass picture-light glow. Three large framed client photographs are hung at eye level across the wall like an exhibition: a candid wedding couple portrait in natural light, a black-and-white family portrait, an engagement photo at golden hour, each with thin white archival mats and a small white museum placard beside it (placard text in tiny grotesk: "Nour & Karim — Engagement / 38 photographs / Delivered 2 Oct 2026").
3. Left of the photographs, large clean grotesk headline set like exhibition wall text in off-white (#f4f6f3): "Every delivery, a private view." Below it one sentence in smaller type: "Hang the shoot in a private gallery, send the link and password on WhatsApp, and see every view and download." Then a solid sage (#c7dcc9) button "Request access" with dark green text and a text link "See a client gallery →".
4. A thin horizontal brass rule at the bottom of the hero with three small wall-text facts in uppercase tracking: "PRIVATE LINK + PASSWORD", "OPEN FOR 7 DAYS", "PROOF OF EVERY DOWNLOAD".
5. Below the fold, partially visible: a white (#f4f6f3) room with a clean three-column "How it works" sequence numbered like exhibition rooms "Room 01 Upload", "Room 02 Share", "Room 03 Proof".

Style: premium, sleek, restrained museum wayfinding typography (neo-grotesk sans, generous tracking on small labels, no serifs), only two inks: forest green and one warm brass accent (#b89a64), matte, calm, generous negative space, real photographic content in the frames. Crisp UI rendering, readable text, no lorem ipsum, no stock watermark, no fake customer logos, no testimonials.
